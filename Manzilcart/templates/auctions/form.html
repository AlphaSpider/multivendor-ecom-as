How auctions work

  • Bidders can place bids while the auction is live. Amounts are public; bidder identities are not shown to other bidders.
  • The seller must explicitly lock the current highest eligible bid before the scheduled end. There is no automatic winner at the end.
  • If you never lock a bid, the auction closes unsold.
  • After you lock a bid, bidding stops. That bidder has {{ confirmation_hours }} hour{{ confirmation_hours|pluralize }} to confirm or decline.
  • If they decline or time out, the next highest eligible bidder is offered the auction with a fresh deadline. Each bidder is offered the auction at most once.
  • The final deal is always at the winning bidder's own bid price. Payment and handover are arranged directly between you and the buyer.
  • Bids below the optional reserve price can still be placed, but cannot be locked as the winner.
